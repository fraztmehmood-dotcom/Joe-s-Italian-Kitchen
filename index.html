Welcome to Joe's

Located in the beautiful historic town of Almonte, Ontario, we're nestled away in a heritage Woolen Mill. Sit back, relax, and soak in the rustic charm while we bring you a diverse menu that weaves old world tradition with new world creativity.

  • Outdoor seating on the river
  • Great cocktails & local craft beer
  • Vegan & gluten-free options available
